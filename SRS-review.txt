P95 [List Paragraph]: Laravel provides a variety of tools and features for developing web applications, including routing, middleware, controllers, models, views, and authentication. It follows the Model-View-Controller (MVC) architectural pattern and emphasizes convention over configuration, which means that developers can get up and running quickly without having to spend a lot of time configuring the framework.

P99 [List Paragraph]: Visual Studio serves as the primary integrated development environment (IDE). It supports PHP and Laravel development workflows, including debugging, testing, and source control integration, ensuring high developer productivity.
