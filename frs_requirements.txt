BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS
Goal and Objectives
To provide a centralized platform for managing policy and circular documents so that all organizational policies and circulars are stored in a single repository, making them easier to manage, access, and maintain.
To improve operational efficiency and reduce manual processes by digitizing policy and circular management activities, minimizing paperwork, and reducing document duplication.
To improve accessibility of policy and circular information by enabling authorized users to search, view, and retrieve documents quickly and efficiently.
To facilitate the creation, updating, and publication of policy circulars by providing a structured process for managing document information and related files throughout their lifecycle.
