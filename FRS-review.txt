P128 [Heading 2]: Goal and Objectives
P130 [List Paragraph]: To provide a centralized platform for managing policy and circular documents so that all organizational policies and circulars are stored in a single repository, making them easier to manage, access, and maintain.
P131 [List Paragraph]: To improve operational efficiency and reduce manual processes by digitizing policy and circular management activities, minimizing paperwork, and reducing document duplication.
P132 [List Paragraph]: To improve accessibility of policy and circular information by enabling authorized users to search, view, and retrieve documents quickly and efficiently.
P133 [List Paragraph]: To facilitate the creation, updating, and publication of policy circulars by providing a structured process for managing document information and related files throughout their lifecycle.
